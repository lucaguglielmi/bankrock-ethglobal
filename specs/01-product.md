# Product specification

## One-sentence pitch

Bank Rock is a handmade physical object that owns and operates onchain liquidity: tap it to see, fund, trade with or receive the rock through a familiar Privy-powered experience.

## Core proposition

Most crypto products begin with a wallet interface and ask the user to understand the infrastructure. Bank Rock begins with a tangible object.

Each rock has:

- a permanent public identity;
- a dedicated onchain account or equivalent isolated ownership boundary;
- token balances controlled by its current owner;
- one or more Aqua liquidity strategies;
- a public history of activation, trades, earnings and ownership transfers;
- a dedicated **MCP (Model Context Protocol) Server**, allowing AI agents to converse with the rock, query its liquidity status, and suggest optimal strategies.

The experience should feel like awakening, filling and passing on a living object—not configuring a DeFi account.

## Primary use case

**The giftable market maker**

A giver activates a rock and seeds it with a small token pair. The liquidity can begin operating before the gift is claimed. The recipient later accepts control of the complete rock: its account, liquidity configuration and history.

This is more distinctive than gifting a token balance because the recipient receives an active economic object.

## Secondary use cases

- A physical micro-exchange at an event, exhibition or shop.
- An educational object for explaining liquidity and market making.
- A collectible whose visible history reflects how it has circulated.
- A reusable physical interface for a creator or community treasury.
- An AI-managed portfolio object: the user queries their AI agent to monitor the rock's earnings and get recommendations on strategy adjustments via MCP.

These remain secondary until the primary gift flow works end to end.

## Product principles

1. **Object first:** every important screen identifies the physical rock, not merely a wallet.
2. **No seed phrase onboarding:** Privy hides wallet creation and recovery behind familiar authentication.
3. **Self-custodial control:** the owner retains control; neither the NFC tag nor the Bank Rock server can withdraw funds.
4. **Public to inspect, private to control:** anyone may view a rock, but only its authenticated owner can change it.
5. **Financial honesty:** show fees, risks and balances precisely. Do not promise APY or guaranteed growth.
6. **Tangible history:** activation, strategies, trades and transfers should make the object feel persistent.
7. **Small-value friendly:** the initial experience should work with tiny balances and sponsored or minimized transaction friction.

## Success measures for the hackathon

- A first-time visitor can tap a rock and understand it within 15 seconds.
- A new user can obtain a Privy wallet without prior crypto setup.
- A rock can expose a real Aqua-backed liquidity strategy.
- A second user can execute a real testnet interaction against the rock.
- Ownership cannot be gained by copying the NFC URL.
- The demonstration clearly explains why Aqua and Privy are necessary.
