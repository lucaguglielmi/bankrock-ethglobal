# Post-Hackathon Roadmap & Exploratory Ideas

This document captures advanced architectural concepts, hardware-level security integrations, and long-term product ideas deferred beyond the initial hackathon MVP scope.

---

## 1. Physical NFC as a 2FA Co-Signer (Hardware-Enforced Multi-Sig)

### Context & Threat Model
In the hackathon architecture, Privy provides embedded wallet key management, while the NTAG 424 DNA tag provides proof of physical presence via cryptographic SUN (Secure Unique NFC) signatures during web interactions.

However, if an attacker compromises a user's web session or recovery credentials (e.g., social login, email auth), they could theoretically liquidate the Rock Account remotely without physically holding the rock.

### Proposal: 2-of-2 Smart Account Security
Elevate the ERC-4337 Smart Account to require multi-factor or multi-signer verification for high-risk operations:
* **Routine Operations (Tier 1):** Viewing state, changing presentation metadata, or automated rebalancing via scoped session keys only require standard Privy auth or session key permissions.
* **High-Risk Operations (Tier 2):** Full balance liquidation, external asset withdrawals, or transferring controller ownership require two distinct signatures:
  1. **Privy User Signature:** Proves user intent and digital authorization.
  2. **Physical NFC Cryptographic Proof:** Generated by a physical tap.
     * *Phase A (Server-Validated Tap):* The backend verifies a fresh NTAG 424 DNA SUN / AES-128 CMAC tap proof and co-signs as a validating guardian.
     * *Phase B (Hardware Onchain Signer):* Utilizing an NFC chip capable of onchain elliptic curve signing (e.g., Kong Cash / HaLo chips or Infineon SECORA Blockchain applets) as a direct native co-signer on the ERC-4337 Smart Account.

### Benefit
The rock becomes a true physical bearer instrument: **you cannot steal the funds without physically holding the rock.**

## 2. "Dead Man's Switch" & Timelocked Inactivity Social Recovery

### Context
Bank Rock is designed to be gifted and displayed as an aesthetic object. A common risk with consumer crypto gifts is abandonment: a recipient puts the rock on a bookshelf, forgets their email/passkey credentials, or stops interacting with it entirely, leaving the capital permanently stranded.

### Proposal: Inactivity Guardian Recovery Module
Attach an ERC-4337 recovery plugin (or ERC-7579 executor module) to the Rock Account:
* **Heartbeat Mechanism:** Any UserOperation signed by the owner (or an explicit "heartbeat" tap) resets the inactivity timer.
* **Inactivity Trigger:** If no authenticated UserOperation is registered for a configurable inactivity threshold (e.g., 6 months or 1 year), the account enters a **Recovery Eligible** state.
* **Reclaim Sequence:**
  1. The original creator/gifter (or a designated trusted recovery guardian / multisig) can initiate a reclaim request.
  2. A **14-day grace period** begins. Notification alerts (if configured) are dispatched to the current owner.
  3. If the current owner taps the rock or executes any UserOp during the 14-day window, the reclaim sequence is immediately cancelled and the heartbeat is refreshed.
  4. If the grace period expires without owner intervention, control of the Rock Account reverts to the guardian or original gifter, salvaging the deposited liquidity.

### Benefit
Prevents permanently lost funds, protects capital in consumer gifting flows, and builds long-term confidence for users gifting significant token amounts to crypto newcomers.
