# Infographic Plan 3: The Bank Rock Stack (What we build on top of Aqua)

**Title:** Beyond the Pool: The Bank Rock Tech Stack
**Theme:** Showing how Aqua is the engine, but Bank Rock builds a complete, frictionless, AI-driven vehicle around it using cutting-edge infrastructure.

## Visual Flow & Sections

### 1. The Core Engine: 1inch Aqua
- **Visual:** A glowing blue core at the bottom center representing Aqua. 
- **Text/Concept:** Aqua provides the base liquidity infrastructure—virtual balances and immutable strategies. But raw DeFi is hard for normal users to interact with.

### 2. The Smart Account Layer: Pimlico (ERC-4337)
- **Visual:** A protective shield or "chassis" wrapping around the Aqua core.
- **Text/Concept:** We use **Pimlico** to power ERC-4337 Smart Accounts for every Bank Rock. 
  - **Gasless Magic:** A Paymaster sponsors transaction fees for new users, so they don't need ETH to start.
  - **Self-Sustaining Rocks:** The rock can pay its own gas fees directly from the trading fees it earns in Aqua.

### 3. The Automation Layer: Gelato & Session Keys
- **Visual:** Robotic arms or gears attached to the smart account, spinning autonomously.
- **Text/Concept:** Using **Gelato** (or similar relay/automation networks) combined with ERC-7579 Session Keys, the rock becomes autonomous. It can harvest yield or rebalance its Aqua strategies automatically without the owner needing to sign every transaction.

### 4. Yield & Cross-Chain Superpowers: Aave & Across/LayerZero
- **Visual:** Pipelines pulling in energy from external clouds (representing other chains and yield protocols).
- **Text/Concept:** 
  - **Idle Yield:** Tokens sitting in the rock aren't just waiting to be traded; they are automatically deployed into **Aave** or **Morpho** to earn passive yield.
  - **Cross-Chain Intents:** Using **Across** or **LayerZero**, a visitor can tap a rock on Base and buy tokens using their funds on Arbitrum seamlessly in one click.

### 5. The Intelligence Layer: AI Oracle (MCP)
- **Visual:** A digital "brain" floating above the rock, analyzing data streams.
- **Text/Concept:** Bank Rock exposes a **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** server. Your personal AI agent (like ChatGPT or Claude) can connect directly to your rock, analyze its Aqua strategy performance, and automatically suggest or execute rebalances.

### 6. The Human Interface: Privy & NFC
- **Visual:** The physical Bank Rock object and a smartphone at the very top of the stack.
- **Text/Concept:** A physical tap (NFC) instantly links the real world to the blockchain. **Privy** handles seamless social logins and embedded wallets, completely hiding the complex stack underneath from the end user.
