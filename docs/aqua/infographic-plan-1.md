# Infographic Plan 1: Why Bank Rock uses Aqua

**Title:** Liquid Object: Why Bank Rock Runs on Aqua
**Theme:** Focus on the contrast between fragmented DeFi and unified, tangible liquidity. Emphasize self-custody and capital efficiency.

## Visual Flow & Sections

### 1. The Old Way: Fragmented & Invisible (The Problem)
- **Visual:** A standard wallet (maybe a digital phone screen) with money flowing out of it and being locked into separate, disconnected "vaults" (representing different AMM pools like Uniswap, Curve, etc.).
- **Text/Concept:** In traditional DeFi, your tokens leave your wallet. Liquidity is fragmented, locked in isolated pools. You lose the tangible sense of ownership.

### 2. The Bank Rock Way: The Object is the Vault (The Solution)
- **Visual:** A beautiful, glowing physical Bank Rock object. Tokens are inside the rock or closely orbiting it, showing they never leave.
- **Text/Concept:** Bank Rock uses the 1inch Aqua protocol. Your capital stays in the Rock's account. It's a self-custodial, physical market maker.

### 3. What Aqua Does Well for Bank Rock
- **Visual:** Three interconnected nodes branching out from the Bank Rock, representing different strategies (e.g., AMM, Fixed Price, Limit Order). A single pool of liquid inside the rock supplies all three simultaneously.
- **Key Points to Highlight:**
  - **Shared Liquidity:** One token balance powers multiple trading strategies at the same time.
  - **Capital Efficiency:** No need to split your funds into tiny, separate deposits. 
  - **True Self-Custody:** Tokens stay in the Rock's wallet until a trade actually happens. The Rock holds the money, Aqua holds the rules.

### 4. The Result: A Living Economic Object
- **Visual:** A person gifting the Bank Rock to another person. The rock glows with active strategies. 
- **Text/Concept:** Because the liquidity isn't scattered across DeFi protocols, the Rock itself retains all its value and history. You can hand someone a fully functioning, physical liquidity pool.
